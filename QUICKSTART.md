# ZEROFY - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 Getting Started in 5 Minutes

### Step 1: Open the Website
1. Navigate to your project folder: `c:\Users\Aditya\OneDrive\Desktop\qq`
2. Double-click on **`index.html`**
3. Your default browser will open with ZEROFY loaded!

### Step 2: Explore the Features
- **Scroll down** to see the interactive animated background with particles and stock charts
- **Check the navbar** - notice the "Login" button
- **Read the floaters** - they explain stock market concepts in simple language

### Step 3: Create an Account
1. Click **"Login"** button in the top-right navbar
2. Click **"Sign Up"** link at the bottom of the modal
3. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name: Your name
   - Email: Any email address
   - Password: Minimum 6 characters
4. Click **"Sign Up"**
5. ✅ You're now logged in! Notice "Welcome, [Your Name]" appears in the navbar

### Step 4: Add Stocks to Portfolio
1. Scroll to the **"Stocks"** section
2. Browse the trending stocks (Apple, Microsoft, Google, etc.)
3. Click **"Add to Portfolio"** on any stock you like
4. Confirm the action - stock is added to your portfolio!

### Step 5: Explore Other Sections
- **Home Page**: Read about ZEROFY's mission
- **Analyse**: View market analysis tools
- **Premium**: Check out premium subscription plans
- **Logout**: Click your name → "Logout" to exit (or stay logged in)

## 💾 Your Data

### Where is it Saved?
Your user data is stored in your browser's **Local Storage**:
- Sign-up information
- Portfolio data
- Saved stocks
- User session

### Can I See My Data?
Yes! Open your browser's Developer Tools:
1. Press **F12** or **Right-click → Inspect**
2. Go to **Application** or **Storage** tab
3. Click **Local Storage**
4. Find and click the domain URL
5. Look for `zerofyUsers` - that's your data!

### Will It be Saved If I Close the Browser?
Yes! Your data persists even after closing. That's why you stay logged in!

## 🐛 Common Issues & Solutions

### ❌ Stocks not showing?
**Solution**: 
1. Refresh the page (Ctrl + F5)
2. Check if JavaScript is enabled
3. Clear browser cache

### ❌ Can't login?
**Solution**:
1. Make sure email is valid
2. Password is at least 6 characters
3. Try creating a new account

### ❌ Portfolio data disappeared?
**Solution**:
1. Check if you cleared browser data
2. Look in browser console (F12)
3. Try signing up and adding stocks again

### ❌ Background animation is slow?
**Solution**:
1. Update your browser
2. Close other tabs/apps
3. Disable browser extensions
