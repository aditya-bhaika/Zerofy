Meet Our Co-Founders

ZEROFY was built by two operators who care about transparency in markets: one from quantitative research and product, the other from investor education and design. Together they focus on tools that explain why prices move — not just what the tick says.

Co-founder & Head of Research

Aditya Bhaika

Aditya leads ZEROFY’s Computational direction: screening logic, factor-style views, and how we translate noisy market data into calm, readable takeaways. Previously he built analytics dashboards for retail investors and mentored university trading clubs on risk-aware position sizing.

Co-founder & Head of Product

Anonymous

Anonymous shapes the ZEROFY experience end-to-end — from onboarding flows to the FinBot assistant and premium education tracks. He believes great analysis should feel like a conversation: structured, jargon-light, and respectful of every skill level.
