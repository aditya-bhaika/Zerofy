What is Stock Market?

Think of the stock market as a huge marketplace where people buy and sell small pieces of companies. When you own a stock, you own a tiny part of that company. When the company does well, your stock value increases!

How Does Stock Market Work?

1

Companies Go Public

Companies decide to sell shares (small pieces) to the public to raise money for growth and expansion.

2

People Buy Shares

Investors like you buy these shares through stock exchanges. More buyers = higher stock price!

3

Stock Prices Change

Stock prices go up and down based on company performance, market news, and investor sentiment.

4

You Earn Money

Sell your stocks when the price is high, and you make a profit. Plus, some companies pay dividends!
