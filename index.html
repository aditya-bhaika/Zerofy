How Does Stock Market Work?

1

Companies Go Public

Companies decide to sell shares (small pieces) to the public to raise money for growth and expansion.

2

People Buy Shares

Investors like you buy these shares through stock exchanges. More buyers = higher stock price!

3

Stock Prices Change

Stock prices go up and down based on company performance, market news, and investor sentiment.

4

You Earn Money

Sell your stocks when the price is high, and you make a profit. Plus, some companies pay dividends!

Why Buy Gold?

Gold is often used as a store of value during inflation, currency weakness, and market uncertainty. Investors add it to balance risk when stocks become volatile.

Why Buy Silver?

Silver has both investment demand and industrial use in electronics, solar panels, and manufacturing. It can move faster than gold, but it also carries higher volatility.
